Buildings: types, materials, qualities and meanings

1Types of buildings les types de bâtiments

Residential buildingsbâtiments résidentiels
houses, apartments, villas…
Institutional buildingsbâtiments institutionnels
schools, colleges, courts…
Commercial buildingsbâtiments commerciaux
shops, malls, supermarkets…

2Materials: concrete les matériaux : le béton

Concrete (le béton) is a mix of sand, water, cement, gravel and crushed stones.

3Buildings have to be… un bâtiment doit être…

functionalfonctionnel
it works well for its use
aesthetically pleasingbeau, agréable à regarder
invitingaccueillant
it makes you want to come in
practicalpratique
easy to use every day

They are designed for the space users = the space occupants (les usagers, les occupants de l'espace).

4Why do we need buildings? pourquoi construire ?

Shelter (un abri): to protect people from

  • natural calamities catastrophes naturelles (earthquakes, floods…)
  • inclement weather le mauvais temps (rain, storms, heat…)

Comfort (le confort), for example:

  • a curtain wall un mur-rideau: an outer wall made of glass, hung on the structure
  • glazed vitré = fitted with glass

6What are the messages behind buildings? le sens des bâtiments

A building is not only a construction: it has a meaning. (un bâtiment a un sens)

A building speaks volumes about…
un bâtiment en dit long sur…

Skyscrapers (gratte-ciel) are built to display (montrer) power and wealth.

people's culturela culture
people's religionla religion
the climatele climat
wealthla richesse

Learn by heart · à savoir par cœur

  • 3 types: residential, institutional, commercial buildings.
  • Concrete = sand + water + cement + gravel + crushed stones.
  • Buildings have to be functional, aesthetically pleasing, inviting and practical.
  • Buildings give shelter (natural calamities, inclement weather) and comfort.
  • a dwelling (the place) ≠ a dweller (the person) ; openings = doors + windows.
  • A building speaks volumes about culture, religion, climate and wealth.
